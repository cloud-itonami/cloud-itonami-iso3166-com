# Business model — COM

Market-entry compliance for the Union of the Comoros.

- Autorité de Régulation des Marchés Publics (ARMP) public procurement --
  Comores Marchés Publics, a real named e-procurement/online tender-
  management system (décret n°23-080/PR du 15 août 2023), governed by
  the Code des Marchés Publics 2022 (see `src/marketentry/facts.cljk`)
- Agence Nationale pour la Promotion des Investissements (ANPI, guichet
  unique) company/business registration; RCCM certificate issued by the
  Tribunal de Commerce under directly-applicable OHADA Uniform Acts
- Administration Générale des Impôts et des Domaines (AGID) tax
  registration (carte du contribuable / carte de patente), Loi
  n°12-004/AU
- Code des Marchés Publics 2022 Art.75 7°: an ARMP exclusion decision
  bars a bidder from public procurement unless it has already
  prescribed (lapsed) after five years -- this vertical's flagship
  governor check independently recomputes that prescription window
  rather than trusting a pre-computed boolean.
